The problem

When someone searches "plumber near me" or "dentist Irvine," Google shows a map with three businesses at the top, the Map Pack. Those top three results get most of the clicks and calls. If your profile isn't in them, many customers never see you, even if your work is better.

Ranking there isn't luck. It depends on how your profile is set up: categories, services, reviews, activity, and how you compare with nearby competitors.
